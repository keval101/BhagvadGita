Bhagavad Gita

This site presents the Bhagavad Gita as a readable study text: the Sanskrit verse, transliteration, English translation, Hindi meaning, and traditional commentary for every chapter and verse.


Bhagavad Gita, comprised of 700 Sanskrit verses across 18 chapters, is India's single most important literary and philosophical contribution. Its teaching on duty, devotion, knowledge, and the nature of the self remains as relevant today as when it was first spoken and recorded.


Sometimes called Gitopanishad, the Gita is regarded as the essence of the Upanishads and as a foundation of Vedic culture, philosophy, and spiritual practice. Here you can move from a chapter overview into each verse without leaving the original context.
