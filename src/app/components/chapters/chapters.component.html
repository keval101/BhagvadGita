Bhagavad Gita – All 18 Chapters

The Bhagavad Gita is arranged in three groups of six chapters. Chapters 1 to 6 emphasize Karma Yoga, the discipline of action. Chapters 7 to 12 emphasize Bhakti Yoga, the path of devotion. Chapters 13 to 18 emphasize Jnana Yoga, the path of knowledge. Open any chapter to read its summary and every verse.
